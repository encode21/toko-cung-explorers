# Mountain material sources

Both textures are redistributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Downloaded 10 November 2026 as 1024-pixel diffuse JPEGs and converted to WebP quality 86 for local hosting. No runtime external requests.

- `rock-face.webp`: [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03), Dario Barresi (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing). Source: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rock_face_03/rock_face_03_diff_1k.jpg
- `forest-ground.webp`: [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04), Rob Tuytel (photography and processing), Rico Cilliers (minor adjustment). Source: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/forest_ground_04/forest_ground_04_diff_1k.jpg

Adjacent JSON sidecars retain source provenance. Code-generated geometry, materials and animation are authored in this project.
