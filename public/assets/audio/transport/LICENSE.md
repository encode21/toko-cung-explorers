# Transport effects

Recordings from Mixkit, used under the [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/).

- `door-open.mp3` — latch excerpt from “Getting into a car” (1539).
- `door-close.mp3` — “Car door slam” (1564).
- `horn.mp3` — “Small car horn” (717).
- `helicopter.mp3` — excerpt from “Helicopter propellers in the sky” (2704).
- `brake.mp3` — mix of “Motorcycle engine decelerating” (2726) and “Rubber fast squeak” (1013).

- `engine-car.mp3` — sustained engine from “Car ignition” (1535).
- `engine-motorcycle.mp3` — “Motorcycle engine working” (2721).
- `engine-sport-bike.mp3` — sustained engine from “Sports bike working motor” (2729).

Source collections: [Car](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/car/), [Transport](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/transport/), and [Squeak](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/squeak/). The recordings were trimmed, filtered, and encoded as mono MP3 for in-game playback. The engine excerpts were matched and crossfaded at the seam for looping.
